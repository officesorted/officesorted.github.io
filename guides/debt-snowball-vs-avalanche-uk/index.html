Debt Snowball vs Avalanche: Which Is Better? (Worked UK Example)

Updated 16 November 2026

Both methods use the same idea: pay the minimum on every debt, then put every spare pound on one target debt. When it's cleared, its payment "rolls" onto the next. The only difference is the order.

  • Snowball: smallest balance first. You clear debts quickly, which keeps you motivated.
  • Avalanche: highest interest rate (APR) first. Usually costs the least in interest.

Here avalanche saves £36.64 and finishes in the same month, while snowball clears three small debts in the first seven months. The gap gets bigger when your debts have very different interest rates and big balances on the most expensive ones.

Which should you choose?

The best method is the one you'll stick to. If you need quick wins to stay motivated, use snowball. If you're disciplined and want to pay the least, use avalanche. Either beats paying minimums only.

Before you start

  • Keep up priority bills first: rent or mortgage, council tax, energy.
  • Look for a 0% balance transfer if your credit score allows it.
