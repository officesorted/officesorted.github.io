HMRC Mileage Rate 2026/27: 55p a Mile for the Self-Employed

Updated 16 November 2026

If you're self-employed and use your own car or van for work, you can claim simplified expenses for business mileage instead of working out your actual running costs. For the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027) the rates on GOV.UK are:

Vehicle2026/27Before 6 April 2026
Car or van, first 10,000 business miles55p45p
Car or van, after 10,000 miles25p25p
Motorcycle24p24p

Worked example

You drive 12,000 business miles in 2026/27: 10,000 × 55p = £5,500, plus 2,000 × 25p = £500. Total claim: £6,000.

For your 2025/26 return (due 31 January 2027) use the old rate: 45p for the first 10,000 miles.

Rules to know

  • Business journeys only: your normal commute to a permanent workplace doesn't count.
  • If you use the flat rate for a vehicle, you can't also claim its fuel, insurance, repairs or road tax.
  • Parking and tolls for business journeys can still be claimed separately.
  • Keep a mileage log: date, from, to, purpose and miles.
