Sinking Funds: The UK List (and How Much to Save Each Month)

Updated 16 November 2026

A sinking fund is money you put aside a little at a time for a cost you know is coming. Instead of Christmas, the car's MOT or the TV licence landing on a credit card, the money is already waiting.

The formula

Save per month = (target − already saved) ÷ months left

£600 for Christmas with 12 months to go is £50 a month. Start in March and it's £66.67 a month, which is why starting early matters.

UK sinking funds list

FundWhy
ChristmasThe biggest yearly spend for most families
Birthdays & giftsSpread through the year, easy to forget
Car: MOT, service, tyresUsually all at once
Car insurance & road taxIf you pay yearly (often cheaper than monthly)
TV licenceIf you pay in one go

PetsVet bills, insurance excess
Emergency fundStart with one month of essential bills

Where to keep the money

Many UK banking apps let you create named pots or "spaces" inside your account. Named pots work better than one general savings account, because "Spain 2027" is harder to spend than "savings". An easy-access savings account with interest is another option for bigger pots.

Keep it simple

  • Start with three pots: Christmas, car, emergency
  • Pay in on payday, by standing order
  • Check once a month and adjust
