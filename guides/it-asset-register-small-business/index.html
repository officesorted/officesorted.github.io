IT Asset Register for a Small Business: What to Record (Free Checklist)

Updated 16 November 2026

An IT asset register is simply a list of every device and piece of software your business pays for, who has it, and when it needs attention. It's the cheapest way to stop losing laptops, missing warranty repairs and paying for software nobody uses.

What to record for every device

FieldWhy it matters
Device type & modelPlan replacements in batches
Serial numberWarranty claims, insurance, theft reports
Assigned toGet it back when people leave
Purchase date & costAge, budget and depreciation
Warranty end dateGet repairs done free
StatusIn use, spare, broken, disposed

What to record for software and subscriptions

  • Product and plan
  • Monthly or annual cost
  • Number of licences
  • Renewal date
  • Owner (one named person)
  • Last used

Keeping it up to date

  • Update it whenever someone joins or leaves
  • Check warranties 90 days ahead
  • Review subscriptions 30 days before each renewal
  • Do a full check once a quarter

A spreadsheet is enough for most businesses under 50 people. Dedicated IT asset management tools make sense later.
