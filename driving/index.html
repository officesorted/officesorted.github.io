Questions

Does it include theory test questions?

No. Official DVSA theory questions and hazard perception clips need a licence, so the planner contains none. It tracks your revision and mock scores from any official or licensed app.

Are the fees up to date?

They were checked on GOV.UK in November 2026 and you can edit them. Fees change, so always check GOV.UK before booking.

Is it for learners or parents?

Both. Learners track skills and revision; parents track the cost of lessons and practice against a budget.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes: sheets.google.com, then File > Import > Upload.
